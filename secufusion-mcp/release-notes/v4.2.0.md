## Summary
`/sfn-review` is rewritten as a standalone, product-aware, investigation-driven reviewer. It builds its own product knowledge (`.mcp/`, the project spec, the DNA graph, then the source), works on any target, and reviews the change against the product and not only the code.

## Added
- Eight review dimensions: code quality, product behavior and feature contracts, service boundaries, deployment model, tenant hierarchy, privacy, operational resilience, future maintainability.
- PASS 11, Product & Privacy Fit: five questions (product behavior, production deployment, tenant hierarchy, six-month maintenance, privacy) answered as FITS, DOES NOT FIT, CONCERN or UNVERIFIED, each with its source.
- PASS 12 to 18: product behavior and contracts, service boundaries, deployment model, tenant hierarchy, privacy model, operational resilience, future maintainability.
- Hypothesis-driven method: the reviewer models the change and its blast radius, forms specific hypotheses, investigates each, and writes a Reasoning trace that includes the suspicions it dismissed.
- New report sections: Product knowledge used, Review dimensions, Product & privacy fit, Reasoning trace, Knowledge gaps.

## Changed
- The review accepts any work-item type verbatim (`BUG-1230`, `TASK-1230`, ...), a file, a folder, a service or a git diff.
- PASS 0 to 10 are unchanged (PASS 10, diff quality, keeps its name and behavior).
- The reviewer persona describes the investigative method and the evidence rule.

## Fixed
- None in this release.

## Breaking changes
None. No tool was added, removed or renamed; existing commands keep working.

## New tools
None.

## Notes
- The reviewer never invents product rules. Product findings state a Source and a Basis; only a FACT can block; a rule nobody recorded is reported as UNVERIFIED and listed under Knowledge gaps. Recording the project's tenant hierarchy, privacy model and deployment model in `.mcp/` is the quickest way to improve reviews.
